CONSERVATION PLAN OF OPERATIONS
Producer: Willow Bend Farm          County: Rockingham, Virginia
Planner: Shenandoah Valley SWCD     Plan year: 2026
Total farm acreage: 214

CURRENT LAND USE
  Cropland, corn/soybean rotation ................ 180 ac
  Permanent pasture .............................. 22 ac
  Farmstead and lanes ............................ 12 ac
  Perennial stream frontage ...................... 2,400 linear ft

PRACTICES CURRENTLY IMPLEMENTED
  Nutrient management plan, whole farm ........... 180 ac   (EQIP, 2024)
  Conservation tillage on cropland ............... 140 ac   (producer funded)

PRACTICES PLANNED
  Pasture planting, convert cropland ............. 120 ac   (planned 2027)
  Riparian forest buffer along perennial stream .. 9 ac, 60 ft average width
  Alternative watering facility, two troughs ..... planned with buffer
  Perimeter fencing .............................. 4,200 linear ft

RESOURCE CONCERNS
  Sheet and rill erosion on cropland slopes above the stream.
  Livestock currently have direct stream access in the pasture block.
  No shade in the proposed pasture; producer has asked about tree planting.

NOTES
  Producer is interested in rotational grazing but has not committed to a
  move schedule. Water infrastructure is the limiting factor; nearest well
  is 900 ft from the proposed paddocks.
